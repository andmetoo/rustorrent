# Rustorrent

## Register
product

## Users
People downloading and sharing files with BitTorrent, primarily on a Mac laptop. They want to add a torrent, choose a destination, follow progress, and understand whether a transfer needs attention without learning protocol terminology.

## Product Purpose
A compact, self-contained torrent client with a native Mac window and a local browser interface. A first beta must reliably download, seed, pause, resume, restart, and remove transfers while protecting existing files.

## Brand Personality
Calm, clear, capable. Use short, concrete labels and explain waiting states honestly.

## Anti-references
Avoid a dashboard of repeated statistics, large decorative cards, unexplained peer counters, and presenting normal seeding inactivity as an error. Avoid hiding routine actions behind technical settings.

## Design Principles
- Make the transfer list the main workspace.
- Distinguish downloading, waiting, paused, completed, and seeding.
- Keep advanced controls discoverable through progressive disclosure.
- Show meaningful failures with a recovery action.
- Preserve keyboard focus, user input, and scroll position during live updates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, sufficient contrast, and narrow screens. State must remain understandable without color.
