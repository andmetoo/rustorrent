# Rustorrent 0.1.0 Beta 2

A full audit and performance pass, a redesigned interface, and a command line that can do everything the window can.

![Rustorrent 0.1.0 Beta 2](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/josusanmartin/rustorrent/v0.1.0-beta.2/docs/screenshots/hero-light.png)

## Download

- **Apple silicon:** `Rustorrent-0.1.0-beta.2-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel or Apple silicon:** `Rustorrent-0.1.0-beta.2-universal.dmg`
- **Linux x86_64:** `rustorrent-0.1.0-beta.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (needs OpenSSL 3)
- ZIP archives of the app and `SHA256SUMS.txt` are attached below. The app requires macOS 11 or newer.

The app is ad-hoc signed for integrity. It is **not Developer ID signed or Apple notarized**, so on first launch macOS may ask you to allow it: right-click the app, choose **Open**, or allow it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security**.

## New

**Interface**
- **Redesigned library.** A sidebar with filters and labels, and rows that expand into Files, Trackers, Peers and Info tabs.
- **Faster adding.** Drop a `.torrent` anywhere or paste a magnet link. A preview lets you pick files before the download starts.
- **Everyday polish.** Keyboard shortcuts, toast messages, light and dark themes that follow the system, and a layout that works down to phone width.
- **Smooth live updates.** Only what changed is streamed, so typing, focus and scroll position are never interrupted.

**Command line**
- **`rustorrent remote <command>`** controls a running instance from a shell: adding, lifecycle actions, files, labels, trackers, limits, seed ratio, peer profile, search, search plugins and RSS. It supports `--json` output for scripts.
- **A rebuilt terminal interface** (`--tui`) with the same actions. It can attach to a background instance with `rustorrent remote tui`.
- **`--help` and `--version`**, and a magnet link can be passed as the only argument.

## Faster

| | Beta 1 | Beta 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Piece hashing (SHA-1) | 192 MB/s | 1.5 GB/s |
| Download from 4 seeders | 48 MB/s | 164 MB/s |
| Upload to 4 leechers | 210 MB/s | 675 MB/s |
| uTP transfer of 2 MiB | 83 s | 0.5 s |

Tracker announces, resume saves and rechecks no longer stall peers. Piece selection is about 160 times faster on large torrents. The Linux binary shrank from 2.2 MB to 1.6 MB.

## Fixed

**Protocol and peers**
- **uTP** now follows BEP 29 and works with other clients. uTP between Beta 2 and older rustorrent builds does not connect; TCP still works.
- **DHT** now runs proper iterative lookups, so it finds peers and makes you findable.
- **Downloads no longer stall** near the end.
- **Idle peers are dropped.**
- **Unchoking favours your fastest peers** instead of being effectively random.
- **Transmission** now reliably requests from a Beta 2 seed over encrypted connections.

**Library and lifecycle**
- **Stopping or removing works during loading:** a magnet that is still fetching metadata, and a torrent that is still verifying, can now be stopped, paused or removed.
- **Deleting a stale duplicate** can no longer delete an active transfer's files.
- **Seeding and paused transfers** no longer block new downloads.
- **Uploaded torrents** that fail before starting now show their real name.
- **Watch folder:** a `.torrent` file still being copied into it is no longer lost.

**Storage and search**
- **Large torrents on macOS:** torrents with hundreds of files no longer hit the open-file limit.
- **Blocklists:** PeerGuardian and eMule blocklists load.
- **Search plugins:** downloads through a plugin work, and one failing engine no longer stops the others.

**Robustness**
- **Stricter input limits:** stronger limits on tracker, peer and torrent-file input.
- **Startup soon after boot:** the app no longer crashes when launched shortly after the Mac starts up.

## Validation

**Tests**
- **Rust:** 515 unit tests, 9 process tests and 20 transport tests.
- **Real processes:** 8 transfer and crash-recovery scenarios.
- **Transmission interoperability,** including 30 consecutive runs of the encrypted upload.
- **Browser flows** with WCAG A/AA accessibility checks.

**CI** covers Linux, macOS and Windows, Rust 1.89, dependency advisories and the license notice.

The full list of changes, with measurements, is in the [audit report](https://github.com/josusanmartin/rustorrent/blob/v0.1.0-beta.2/docs/AUDIT_REPORT_2026-09-26.md).
